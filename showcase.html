The Physical AI Paradigm

No pure chatbots. No isolated dashboards. Real hardware with physical consequence.

01
📡

Sense

Capturing real-world context through cameras, microphones, ultrasonic transducers, accelerometers, temperature probes, and LiDAR.

02
🧠

Process

Running lightweight models on constrained compute — Google Gemma 3, Gemini Nano/Flash APIs, OpenCV, and MediaPipe at the edge.

03
⚡

Act

Triggering mechanical servos, solenoid valves, haptic actuators, stepper motors, LED arrays, or electronic relays in physical space.
